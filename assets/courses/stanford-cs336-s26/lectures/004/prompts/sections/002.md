Linear attention
Consider the usual attention operation.. 𝑄∈𝑅𝑛×𝑑𝑘, 𝐾∈𝑅𝑛×𝑑𝑘, 𝑉∈𝑅𝑛×𝑑𝑣
𝐴𝑡𝑡𝑛𝑄, 𝐾, 𝑉= 𝜌𝑄𝐾⊤𝑉
This is quadratic due to 𝑄𝐾⊤ (𝑛2𝑑𝑘). Can we do better (when 𝜌 is the identity)?
𝑄𝐾⊤𝑉= 𝑄(𝐾⊤𝑉)
