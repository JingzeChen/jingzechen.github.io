正文：
One approach for finding optimal (N, D)
1.
Pick, say, 4 model sizes: 100M, 300M, 1B, 3B. 
2.
Per size, launch training runs with different LR decay schedules (for, say, D = 20B, 60B, 
180B tokens). Record <compute FLOPs, possibly training loss> throughout training. 
3.
Plot these values and find the lowest achievable loss at any compute C. 
4.
Fit univariate power law for “optimal N vs. C” and/or “optimal D vs. C”. 
5.
Extrapolate both to get optimal (N, D) for a given C. (Or use D ~= C/6N.)

标题：Simplest algorithm: Rejection Fine-Tuning (RFT)
正文：
Simplest algorithm: Rejection Fine-Tuning (RFT) 
(aka filtered behavior cloning or guess-and-check; many other names)
• With your current model, for each prompt x, sample K responses. 
• Score each with R(x, y_i). Keep only responses with high reward. 
• Run an “offline” SFT job on the resulting <x, y> pairs. 
• Improvement: Online RFT. For a batch of prompts, sample rollouts 
and take an SFT gradient step to update the model. 
• This can actually take you rather far for many tasks…
